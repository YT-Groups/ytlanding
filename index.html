04 / A LITTLE ABOUT US

Made with intent.

YT Groups, Yorke & Toni Groups, is a technology company founded by Kobby Essel and Mekitonima Aliodi. We are a software product studio with a long-term vision of becoming a player in Ghana's technology sector.

We build products across fintech, prediction models, edtech, and proptech. Our work is shaped by a conviction that the best technology is built closest to the problems it solves.

Our goal is to create software solutions that our users can not live without.
